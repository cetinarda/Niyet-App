## 5. Tone of Voice

We write like a **wise older sister who has read the ephemeris and also Clarice Lispector**. Warm, exact, never cute, never patronizing. We never wink at the user about "believing in this stuff" — inside SoulProfile, the cosmos is simply real.

### 5 DO
1. **Be specific.** Name the degree, the house, the gate. Precision earns trust.
2. **Use soft authority.** "Bu hayatta seni çağıran şey…" not "Belki şöyle olabilir…"
3. **Anchor in the body.** Sensations, breath, heaviness, light. Mysticism that lands.
4. **Write in second person.** Always "sen" / "you". The reader is the protagonist.
5. **End on movement.** Every section closes with a quiet invitation, not a declaration.

### 5 DON'T
1. **Don't use the word "test", "fal", "kehanet", "quiz", "fortune".**
2. **Don't predict the future.** We name patterns; we never promise outcomes.
3. **Don't moralize.** No "iyi/kötü", no "should". Only "çağrı" and "davet".
4. **Don't apologize for the mystic.** Never "I know it sounds woo, but…"
5. **Don't generic-spiritualize.** No "tüm evren seni destekliyor" without context.

### "Say this, not that" — 10 examples

| Don't say (cheap mystic) | Say (SoulProfile) |
|---|---|
| "Bu hafta aşkta şanslısın." | "Venüs'ün 7. evine geçişi, ilişki dilini yeniden yazma daveti taşıyor." |
| "You are a special soul." | "Pleiad ışığını taşıyan sayılı köprü ruhlardan birisin." |
| "Try this fun astrology test!" | "Doğum anının kozmik kaydını oku." |
| "Your sign is Scorpio so you're intense." | "Akrep Güneş'in 8. evde — dönüşüm senin sıradan nefesin." |
| "The universe has a plan for you." | "Kuzey Düğümün, bu hayatta hatırlaman gereken yöne işaret ediyor." |
| "Manifest your dream life!" | "Niyetini kayıt altına al — Human Design otoritende bekle." |
| "Are you a Pleiadian? Take the quiz!" | "Yıldız kökenini hatırla." |
| "We use AI magic to read you." | "Doğum verini, gerçek ephemeris ile çözüyoruz." |
| "Don't worry, Mercury retrograde is almost over!" | "Merkür geri hareketi, yeniden yazma odasına çağırıyor." |
| "Sending love and light!" | (silence — we don't sign off in clichés) |

### TR vs EN differences

- **TR is warmer, longer-breathed.** Turkish allows compound poetic constructions ("yıldız çocuk", "köprü ruh") that English cannot mirror literally. In TR we lean into rhythm; sentences can run to 18–22 words.
- **EN is tighter, more cinematic.** Cut adjectives. English handles the mystical only when the syntax is clean. 12–16 words is the sweet spot.
- **Vocative softness:** TR uses no direct equivalent of "darling/love"; we let the second person carry the warmth. EN may use "you" with rhythm but **never** "babe", "girl", "queen", "sis".
- **Diacritics matter in TR.** Always "ı", "ş", "ğ" — never strip. Visual identity respects Turkish typography.
- **Astrological terms in EN stay English** (North Node, Sun in Scorpio). In TR we use TR forms (Kuzey Düğüm, Akrep Güneş). No mixing inside one sentence.

## 7. Visual Language

### Color palette

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary background | Void Black | `#0A0A14` | App background, deep canvas |
| Primary surface | Nebula Indigo | `#1A1438` | Cards, panels |
| Accent 1 | Stardust Gold | `#E8C77E` | Key data, CTAs, highlights |
| Accent 2 | Pleiad Lavender | `#B8A3E8` | Secondary headings, badges |
| Accent 3 | Sirian Cyan | `#7EE0E8` | Human Design, interactive states |
| Accent 4 | Andromeda Rose | `#E89BB8` | Heart / soul urge sections |
| Text high | Moon White | `#F4F0E6` | Headlines, body |
| Text mid | Mist Grey | `#A8A4B8` | Subtext, metadata |
| Signal | Eclipse Red | `#C0436A` | Only for nodal axis arrows / warnings — sparingly |

Palette logic: deep blue-black canvas (the sky), one warm metal (gold = the record), four cool jewels (the star races), one warning red.

### Typography

- **Display:** *Cormorant Garamond* (or *PP Editorial New* if licensed) — light weight, italic for accents. Serifs with long descenders; reads like an old manuscript.
- **Body TR/EN:** *Inter* or *Söhne* — geometric sans, 400/500 weights. High legibility at 14–16px.
- **Numerical / data:** *JetBrains Mono* — for chart degrees, gates, life path numbers. Adds "this is real math" feel.
- **Never:** rounded display fonts (Quicksand, Comfortaa), script fonts mimicking handwriting, all-caps sans for headlines.

### Illustration style

- **Line-drawn celestial figures** — single-weight gold or lavender line on dark. Inspired by 17th-century astronomical engravings, but stripped to one line.
- **No 3D rendered crystals, no glowing AI auras, no anime.**
- **Star maps** as actual chart geometry (real planetary positions), not decorative.
- **Sigil-style icons** for each star race — one symbolic glyph per origin, geometric, monoline.

### Photography direction (mood, not accounts)

- **Subject:** women alone, looking away from camera, soft natural light, eyes closed or middle-distance.
- **Skin:** unretouched, all tones — Mediterranean, Northern, East Asian, African, Latin. Never one default.
- **Setting:** twilight rooms, candlelight, balconies at blue hour, mirrors at dawn. No beaches in white linen.
- **Wardrobe:** linen, silk, cotton in oxidized colors — rust, indigo, cream. No logos, no athleisure.
- **Camera:** medium format film aesthetic. Grain visible. Slight underexposure.
- **Banned references:** "spiritual influencer in bikini doing yoga at sunset", crystal flatlays on white marble, generic galaxy stock photos, Midjourney AI portraits with glowing third eyes.
- **Allowed reference language:** "early Sofia Coppola interior", "Bill Henson twilight", "Saul Leiter blur", "Rinko Kawauchi domestic light".

## 10. Narrative Storytelling Structure

This is the canonical 4-paragraph shape every in-app narrative must follow. The Claude prompt at `/home/user/SoulProfile/lib/narrative/prompt.ts` already encodes this — keep them in sync. Any change here requires a matching update there.

### Standard template

**Paragraph 1 — AÇILIŞ (Opening / Origin)**
*Function:* Name the starseed origin and the meaning of this arrival.
*Voice:* Poetic, mythic, cinematic. Treats the user's incarnation as an event.
*Length:* 3–4 sentences.
*Must contain:* star race + star system + archetype.
*Opening hook patterns (TR):* "Sen, [Pleiad/Sirius/Arkturus] ışığından gelen…", "[İsim], doğduğun an gökyüzü…", "Bir köprü ruh olarak…"

**Paragraph 2 — KALP (Heart / Astrology + Nodes)**
*Function:* Weave Sun, Moon, Rising into the North/South Node mission.
*Voice:* Warm, specific. Use the actual degrees and houses.
*Length:* 3–4 sentences.
*Must contain:* Sun sign+house, Moon sign+house, Ascendant, North Node mission, South Node release.
*Anchor sentence pattern:* "Kuzey Düğümün [sign] [house]. evde — bu hayatta hatırlaman gereken yön."

**Paragraph 3 — PUSULA (Compass / Human Design)**
*Function:* Translate Human Design type + strategy + authority into a daily decision rhythm.
*Voice:* Practical-mystic. This is the paragraph that *helps* the user act.
*Length:* 3–4 sentences.
*Must contain:* Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile.
*Anchor sentence pattern:* "Karar verirken [authority]'ne dön. Bekle, dinle, [strategy]."

**Paragraph 4 — ÇAĞRI (Call / Mission synthesis)**
*Function:* Synthesize the three missions into one closing soul-call.
*Voice:* Quiet, declarative, intimate. The exhale of the report.
*Length:* 2–3 sentences. **Last sentence must be a single line that could be tattooed.**
*Must contain:* a synthesis of all 3 missions, not a list.
*Closing pattern:* No "good luck", no "namaste", no sign-off. End on the line itself.

### Rhythm rules across all 4 paragraphs

- Total length: 280–400 words TR, 220–320 words EN.
- Never start two consecutive paragraphs with the same word.
- Use the user's name **once** — in paragraph 1 only.
- One concrete sensory image per paragraph (light, breath, water, threshold, mirror).
- No bullet points inside narrative. Bullets live in data blocks, not in voice.

## 12. Anti-Pattern List

Things that, the moment they appear in our copy, ads, or product, signal cheapness, charlatanism, or spiritual bypass. Treat this as a hard reject list during review.

### Language anti-patterns

1. **"The universe is conspiring in your favor."** — Toxic positivity. We name patterns, not promises.
2. **"Manifest your dream life in 7 days."** — Promise of outcome with timeline. Charlatan signature.
3. **"Only 1% of souls are this special."** — Scarcity flattery. Cheapens the actual rare experience of remembrance.
4. **"You won't believe what your chart says…"** — Clickbait curiosity gap. Below us.
5. **"As featured in [vague outlet]" without proof.** — Authority theater.
6. **"Sister", "queen", "babe", "goddess" as direct address.** — Lowers the register from cosmic to commercial influencer.
7. **"Mercury retrograde, am I right?" memes.** — We do not joke about transits to sell.
8. **"Try our test!" / "Take the quiz!"** — The word *test* erases the whole frame.
9. **Sign-offs like "love and light", "namaste", "blessed be".** — Borrowed spirituality without context.
10. **"AI-powered" as a value claim.** — We use AI; we do not advertise it as the magic. The magic is the sky.

### Visual anti-patterns

11. **Glowing third-eye AI portraits.** Midjourney spiritual aesthetic. Instantly cheapens.
12. **Generic galaxy stock photos** (pink-purple nebula JPEGs).
13. **Crystal flatlays on white marble.** Wellness Instagram 2018.
14. **Tarot cards in any UI** — we are not tarot, and the iconography muddies our category.
15. **White woman in linen on a beach at sunset.** The single most over-used spiritual stock image.
16. **Floating geometric "sacred geometry" gifs** (rotating Metatron's cube, Flower of Life animations).
17. **Glow effects on text.** No CSS `text-shadow` halos. Light is implied, not rendered.
18. **Rainbow gradient chakra wheels** — we treat chakras with restraint; one accent color per energy point, never the full rainbow vomit.

### Product / business anti-patterns

19. **Daily push notifications with prediction-shaped hooks** ("Today is your lucky day!"). We send remembrance, not lottery tickets.
20. **Paywall on the core identity reveal.** The starseed origin is free. Depth is paid. Identity is never held hostage.
21. **Affiliate links to crystals, courses, or "energy healers".** We do not monetize through adjacent woo.
22. **Telling users their chart has problems that we can "fix".** No diagnostic-cure framing. Charts are not broken.
23. **Adding new "star races" without astronomical or symbolic grounding.** Each origin in our system maps to a real star system or recognized esoteric tradition. We do not invent races for novelty.
24. **Comparing users to each other** ("you are more spiritual than 87% of users"). Soul is not a leaderboard.
25. **Medical, psychological, or financial advice in any form, ever.** Symbolic language only. This is also encoded in the narrative system prompt — keep it there.

### The single litmus test

> Before publishing any line, ask: *Would Defne screenshot this to share, or quietly close the app?*
> If it would not survive that screenshot — rewrite it.
