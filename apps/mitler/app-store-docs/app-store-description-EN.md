**Sakin Myths**: A symbol-card journaling experience in the footsteps of Carl Jung: archetype, myth and image decks turned into a daily reflection game.

Each day three decks open: **Archetypes**, **Myths**, **Images**. Shake the phone or tap the deck, a card drops from Jung's universal patterns, world mythologies and the alphabet of symbols.

Every card comes with two readings:
🌙 **If Seen in a Dream**, what the symbol may mean if it appears in your dream
☀ **In Waking Life**: what the symbol whispers when it shows up in real life

Each card then turns into a journaling prompt. Over time the cards you collect form your **Myth Map**, your streak grows, and your symbol level levels up.

400+ guided cards · 14+ cultures · Inspired by the work of Carl Jung, Joseph Campbell, Caroline Myss, Carol Pearson and Moore & Gillette.

### Features

• **Three daily deck game**, shake or tap to draw cards
• **Symbol collection**: archetype, myth, image and classic symbol sets (Tarot, Rune, I Ching, Numerology) in one library, presented as **cultural study material** (not prediction)
• **Detail cards**: Essence, Light/Shadow Aspect, Dream/Waking readings, Today's Practice, Affirmation
• **Find Your Guide**: 7-question character quiz or birth-date matcher to surface the archetype closest to you
• **Personal Map**: Numerology derived from name and birth date (Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge)
• **Your Myth Map**: over time the cards you collect reveal your dominant archetypes, myths and traditions
• **Weekly Thematic Guide**: a figure walking beside you for a specific period
• **Archive, Streak & Badges**, every card saved, see your daily streak, unlock badges
• **Symbol Atlas**: search 200+ cards by category or keyword
• **Turkish and English**: bilingual interface

### Privacy

All data stays on your device. No account, no sign-up, no internet required, no ads, no tracking. You can delete all of your data with one tap from the Profile screen, any time.

### About Sakin

Sakin is an ecosystem that brings ritual, reflection and stillness to your daily life. Myths is the **Jungian reading** application of the ecosystem.

### Important

Sakin Myths is not a fortune-telling, therapy or medical-advice tool. It is for self-reflection and journaling only. If you are struggling, please consider seeking professional support.
